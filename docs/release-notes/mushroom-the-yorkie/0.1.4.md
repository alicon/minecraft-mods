# Mushroom the Yorkie 0.1.4

- Mushroom walks back after chasing a squirrel. If his way home stays blocked, he can still use normal pet recovery.
- Nearby monsters wake a sleeping Mushroom. He barks at threats through walls and tries to nip them when he can reach them.
- On a lead, Mushroom sometimes stops for a bathroom break, noses off in a direction, or plants his feet for a second. Keep walking and he'll come along.
- Walk over a thrown Creative toy to pick it off the ground. The original stays in your inventory.
- Squirrels can scramble onto the treetops, hop between nearby crowns, or come down when the gap is too wide. They also plant dropped vanilla saplings, which can grow into real trees.
- The doghouse has a properly peaked roof, the squirrel has a new spawn egg, and the copper food and water bowls are easier to tell apart in the world and in the inventory.

This is an alpha for Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11 and 1.21.1. It requires Fabric Loader and Fabric API. Use the matching version on participating clients and on the server or LAN host.
