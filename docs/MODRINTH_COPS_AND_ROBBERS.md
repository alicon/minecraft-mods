# Cops and Robbers

Cops and Robbers adds a small playable police-and-bank scenario to Minecraft worlds.

Build a police station, place a bank, patrol in a police cruiser, and chase down robbers before they make off with stolen gold. The mod is designed for family worlds where kids want a clear, playful objective without needing a large quest system.

**Yep, 100% vibe-coded with Codex and ChatGPT.** It's a bonding project with my daughter and our neighbors. I'm a software engineer, and we come up with ideas by playing together. For 0.1.4, we drove both vehicles through their new bays in Minecraft, checked the robbery-to-jail loop, and verified that solid walls stop the water cannon.

## Features

- Rideable police cruiser with lights, siren, controller-friendly shortcuts, and creative-mode flight tricks.
- Rideable fire truck with an automatic water cannon that puts out exposed fires ahead of the driver.
- Bank robbers that target nearby banks and can scatter when patrol vehicles approach.
- Police cruiser capture behavior for collecting robbers and dropping them near jail cells.
- Placeable police station kit with a jail cell and cruiser garage.
- Placeable fire station kit with a truck bay and responder crew.
- Placeable bank kit with tellers, a vault, and robber targets.
- Creative spawn eggs for cruisers, fire trucks, cops, robbers, bank tellers, and firefighters.

## Patrol, capture, and rescue

Place the three kits on open ground with room in front of each vehicle bay. Robbers can steal gold from the barred bank vault. Catch them in the cruiser and park on the yellow **Prisoner Drop Off** behind the police station. If a fire breaks out, drive the fire truck toward it and aim the front of the truck at the exposed flames. Firefighters also respond on foot.

| While driving | Default keyboard control | Controlify action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toggle lights | `Z` | Inventory |
| Toggle siren | `X` | Drop |
| Barrel roll in Creative | `C` | Swap hands |
| Loop in Creative | `V` | Pick block |

In Creative, double-tap Jump while driving to switch flight on or off. Use Jump to climb and Sneak to descend.

## Why Cops and Robbers?

This is a lightweight pretend-play mod for family Minecraft sessions. It gives kids a reason to patrol, chase, rescue, and rebuild while keeping the systems simple enough to understand from the first few minutes.

## Requirements

Cops and Robbers must be installed on every participating client and on the server or LAN host.

- Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11
- Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer
- Fabric API for Minecraft 1.21.11
